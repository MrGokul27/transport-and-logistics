FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear guidance regarding custom duties classifications, shipping volume weight, and telemetry access.

Physical weight is measured directly on a scale. Dimensional (volumetric) weight is calculated by multiplying length, width, and height, then dividing by a standard coefficient (usually 5000 for standard ocean and air freight). Carriers charge based on whichever value is larger, as high-volume light cargo occupies container space.

Responsibility depends on the agreed Incoterms (International Commercial Terms) signed in the cargo contract. Under DDP (Delivered Duty Paid), the shipper coordinates duties, while under EXW (Ex Works) or FOB (Free on Board), the receiver arranges customs compliance. Our brokerage assists whichever party is designated.

Standard container spaces are locked 7 - 14 days before a vessel's departure date. However, during peak shipping seasons, we recommend submitting cargo manifests 3 - 4 weeks early to guarantee space and lock in lower spot tariffs.
