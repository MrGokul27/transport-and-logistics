Timeline

Our Corporate Milestones

How Stackly grew from a regional road shipper to an international intermodal logistics authority.

2012 — Foundation

Regional trucking logistics agency serving East Coast lines.

We launched with 5 freight vehicles and a commitment to safe overland shipping. Our local compliance record paved the way for early partnerships.

2017 — Aviation & Sea Expansion

Charter air agreements and maritime broker registry.

We expanded globally, opening ports of entry partnerships in Rotterdam and London. Stackly introduced consolidated sea-cargo pricing models to save shipper overhead costs.

2023 — Smart Digitization

Real-time GPS telemetry client portals and automated sorting.

We completely digitalized fleet telemetry, giving shippers access to climate alerts, live routes, and automated customs document submission.
