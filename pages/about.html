Our Journey

Our Corporate Milestones

How Stackly grew from a regional road shipper to an international intermodal logistics authority.

2012 — Foundation Launch Era
Regional Road Shipping

We launched with 5 freight vehicles and a commitment to safe overland shipping. Our local compliance record paved the way for early partnerships, serving East Coast lines.

2017 — Expansion Global Reach
Maritime Broker & Charter Air Agreements

We expanded globally, opening ports of entry partnerships in Rotterdam and London. Stackly introduced consolidated sea-cargo pricing models to save shipper overhead costs.

2023 — Digitization Tech Integration
GPS Telemetry & Sorting Systems

We completely digitalized fleet telemetry, giving shippers access to climate alerts, live routes, and automated customs document submission, setting standard for intelligent routing.
